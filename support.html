Luto support

Questions, problems, or a report about something you saw in Luto? Email support@dfvstudio.com. We reply within two business days, and review reports of objectionable content within 24 hours.

Delete your account

In the app, go to Profile → Settings → Delete account. It permanently deletes your account and everything you've added. If you signed in with Apple, Luto's access to your Apple ID is revoked as well. If you can't get into the app, email us from the address on your account and we'll delete it for you.

Report or block someone

Tap the ··· menu on any comment, collection or profile to report it or block its author. Blocked people are listed under Settings, where you can unblock them.
