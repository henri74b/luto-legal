Luto privacy policy

Effective date: 11 November 2026 · Contact: support@dfvstudio.com

Luto is a recipe discovery app. This policy explains what we collect, why, who processes it, and how to delete it.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address. If you use Sign in with Apple or Google, we also receive the identifier those services provide, and your name if you choose to share it.
  • Profile: the name, @handle, bio and avatar you set, plus the cuisines you pick during onboarding.
  • What you do in Luto: recipes you save, collections you create or join, comments and likes, the cooks and people you follow, your cook log, your searches, recipes you view, and feedback you give on recipes.
  • Reports and blocks: reports you file and people you block.
  • Usage analytics: app interaction events, tied to your account id, sent to PostHog (hosted in the EU).

We don't collect your location, contacts or payment details, and we don't track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Why

We use this data to run your account, to show you recipes and cooks matched to your tastes, to make shared collections and comments work, to keep the community safe, and to understand how the app is used so we can improve it. We don't sell your data and we don't use it for advertising.

Who processes it

  • Supabase stores the account and app data.
  • PostHog (EU) receives usage analytics.
  • Anthropic (Claude) receives the text you type into Ask Mise and Dish Lens, plus the recipe context needed to answer. It generates the reply.
  • Voyage AI receives search and dish text to compute semantic search results.
  • Apple and Google receive sign-in data, only if you use those options.

AI providers receive only the text needed for the feature you're using. They don't receive your email address.

Other users

Your name, @handle, bio, avatar and public collections, and the comments you post on public collections, are visible to other Luto users. Private collections are visible only to you and the collaborators you invite.

Deleting your data

Go to Profile → Settings → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account and everything tied to it: profile, saves, collections (including shared collections you own), comments, likes, follows, cook log, searches and reports. It also revokes Sign in with Apple, if you used it, and removes your analytics profile. Recipes you added to someone else's collection stay there without your name. Deletion is immediate and can't be undone.

Children

Luto isn't directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the effective date above and, for material changes, tell you in the app.
